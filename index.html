THE MVP

Everything important. Nothing bloated.

ReadyBlue is being designed around the parts of the hiring process applicants repeatedly have to organize, prepare for, and keep track of.

🗺️ Application Roadmap

Select an agency and see major hiring stages in a clear sequence, with links back to official sources.

✓ Document Checklist

Keep employment, residence, reference, education and other background information organized.

🏃 Test & Fitness Prep

Organize written-test resources, fitness goals and preparation around official standards.

🎙️ Interview Practice

Practice communicating clearly with mock questions and structured feedback—not memorized or deceptive answers.

📋 Background Organization

Prepare accurate timelines and records so personal-history paperwork can be completed carefully and truthfully.

📈 Progress Tracking

Know what's complete, what's next and what you still need to prepare.
